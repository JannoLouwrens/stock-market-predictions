# Research Papers & Techniques Implemented - Stock Market Predictions

This document lists the academic research papers and techniques implemented in the Stock Market Predictions project.

---

## Sentiment Analysis & Market Prediction

**1. VADER: A Parsimonious Rule-based Model for Sentiment Analysis**
- Authors: Hutto & Gilbert
- Year: 2014 (ICWSM)
- Link: https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICWSM/article/view/14550
- Implementation: Rule-based sentiment scoring

**2. Contextual String Embeddings for Sequence Labeling (Flair)**
- Authors: Akbik et al.
- Year: 2018 (COLING)
- Link: https://aclanthology.org/C18-1139/
- Implementation: Deep learning sentiment classification

**3. When is a Liability not a Liability?**
- Authors: Loughran & McDonald
- Year: 2011
- Implementation: Financial sentiment lexicons

**4. Twitter mood predicts the stock market**
- Authors: Bollen et al.
- Year: 2011
- Implementation: Social sentiment → price correlation

**5. Deep Learning for Event-Driven Stock Prediction**
- Authors: Ding et al.
- Year: 2015
- Implementation: Event-driven features

**6. Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers**
- Authors: Jegadeesh & Titman
- Year: 1993
- Implementation: Momentum features

**7. Giving Content to Investor Sentiment**
- Authors: Tetlock
- Year: 2007
- Implementation: Media content → returns

**8. The Deflated Sharpe Ratio**
- Authors: Bailey et al.
- Year: 2014
- Implementation: Walk-forward validation
